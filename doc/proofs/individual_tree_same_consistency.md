# Consistency of boolean(false) on tree_same_left_first_differ_right

Claim. With M the program of `tree_same_left_first_differ_right` (H15, left first), the
scored test T = \a. M y a (y free) is consistent with lambda beta eta. It suffices that
lambda beta eta + (M = false) is consistent: that theory proves T = \a. false y a =
\a. a.

## 1. The lemma

The lemma of `individual_path_rebuilt_consistency.md`, Section 1 (the false_only
lemma, v general), with v = false: a set G of closed terms that is closed under one-step
beta eta (G1), whose terms other than false are applications (G2), and that is closed
under replacing an occurrence of a member by false (G3), makes lambda beta eta +
{N = false : N in G} consistent. It remains to give such a G containing M.

## 2. The program

From `contest_hard_clauses.pl`, `contest_clauses.pl` and `prelude_clauses.pl`
(T = \t. \f. t, F = \t. \f. f, And = \a. \b. a b F, the numerals Scott numerals, Eq the
prelude's natural_equal, Y the prelude's fixpoint):

    Same = Y (\self. \a. \b. a LeafCase BranchCase)
      LeafCase   = \la. b (\lb. Eq la lb) (\x. \y. \z. F)
      BranchCase = \la. \lA. \rA. b (\x. F)
                     (\lb. \lB. \rB. And (Eq la lb) (And (self lA lB) (self rA rB)))
    Branch l L R = \lf. \br. br l L R,     Leaf l = \lf. \br. lf l
    A = Y (\s. Branch 1 s (Leaf 5)),       B = Y (\s. Branch 1 s (Leaf 6))
    M = Same A B

(LeafCase and BranchCase have `self` and `b` free, bound by Same's body; the two Eq and
the two And are separate copies of the same terms.)

## 3. Components

Each set is the set of all beta eta reducts of the terms listed (so closed under
reduction):

- S: reducts of Same (the same instances).
- TA, TB: reducts of A, resp. of B (the two spines).
- LA, LB: reducts of Leaf 5, resp. Leaf 6.
- S1: reducts of `\b. Body[a := A0]`, A0 in TA, Body the body of Same's abstraction
  over `a` and `b` with `self` a same instance.
- CL, CB: reducts of LeafCase, resp. BranchCase, with `self` a same instance and `b` in
  TB.
- CB1, CB2: reducts of BranchCase's body after `la := 1`, resp. after `la := 1` and
  `lA := A1` (A1 in TA).
- K1: the reducts of `\x. F`, which is normal.
- C3: reducts of `\lb. \lB. \rB. And (Eq 1 lb) (And (Q A1 lB) (Q L5 rB))`, Q in S,
  A1 in TA, L5 in LA; C31, C32 its bodies after `lb := 1`, resp. after `lb := 1` and
  `lB := B1` (B1 in TB).
- E: reducts of Eq 1 1 (the label check; it reduces to T).
- Zr: reducts of Q L5 L6, Q in S, L5 in LA, L6 in LB (the right subtrees' comparison;
  it reduces to F, 5 being not 6).
- AND: the reducts of And, which is normal.

Abstraction forms, by inspection: an abstraction in S is `\a. S1'` with `S1'[a := A0]`
in S1; an abstraction in S1 is `\b. B'` with `B'[b := B0]` of the form `A0' CL0 CB0`
(A0' in TA, CL0 in CL, CB0 in CB); an abstraction in TA is `\lf. \br. br 1 A1 L5`
(A1 in TA, L5 in LA; the spine unfolds to its branch), and in TB `\lf. \br. br 1 B1 L6`;
an abstraction in CB is `\la. CB1'`, in CB1 `\lA. CB2'`, in CB2 `\rA. D'` with D' after
the substitutions of the form `B0' K1 C30` (B0' in TB, C30 in C3); an abstraction in C3
is `\lb. C31'`, in C31 `\lB. C32'`, in C32 `\rB. D''` with D'' after the substitutions
one of the And forms of the production `a-forms` below; the only abstraction in E is T;
the only abstraction in Zr is F.

## 4. The set G

G is generated by the productions below (m ranges over G, the SLOT; other letters over
the components as named; (Z) marks a production that follows the comparison of the two
spines, (C) a collapse state that reduces to F):

    m ::= Q A0 B0                         Q in S, A0 in TA, B0 in TB     (Z)
        | S10 B0                          S10 in S1                      (Z)
        | A0 CL0 CB0                                                     (Z)
        | (\br. br 1 A1 L5) CB0                                          (Z)
        | CB0 1 A1 L5 | CB10 A1 L5 | CB20 L5                             (Z)
        | B0 K1 C30                       C30 in C3                      (Z)
        | (\br. br 1 B1 L6) C30                                          (Z)
        | C30 1 B1 L6 | C310 B1 L6 | C320 L6                             (Z)
        | a-form                                                         (Z or C, as m)
        | F                                                              (C)
        | I F                                                            (C)

    a-form ::= AND E0 c | (\b. E0 b F) c | E0 c F | (\f. c) F | c        E0 in E
    c      ::= AND m Z0 | (\b. m b F) Z0 | m Z0 F                        Z0 in Zr

(`E0 c F` is `(E0 c) F`, and so on; a `c` standing alone is itself an a-form.)

M = Same A B is the production `Q A0 B0`, so M is in G.

## 5. (G1)

A redex lies inside a component (stays there), inside the slot (induction), or at a
production, which has a root redex only when its operator part is an abstraction:

    Q A0 B0            -> S10 B0                   (Q = \a. S1')
    S10 B0             -> A0' CL0 CB0              (S10 = \b. B')
    A0 CL0 CB0         -> (\br. br 1 A1 L5) CB0    (A0 = \lf. \br. br 1 A1 L5)
    (\br. ...) CB0     -> CB0 1 A1 L5
    CB0 1 A1 L5        -> CB10 A1 L5 -> CB20 L5 -> B0' K1 C30
    B0 K1 C30          -> (\br. br 1 B1 L6) C30    (B0 = \lf. \br. br 1 B1 L6)
    (\br. ...) C30     -> C30 1 B1 L6 -> C310 B1 L6 -> C320 L6 -> an a-form
    AND E0 c           -> (\b. E0 b F) c -> E0 c F
    E0 c F             -> (\f. c) F                (E0 = T, the only abstraction in E)
    (\f. c) F          -> c
    AND m Z0           -> (\b. m b F) Z0 -> m Z0 F
    m Z0 F             -> I F                      (m = F, the only abstraction in G:
                                                    F Z0 -> I)
    I F                -> F

There are no eta redexes at production level: `\b. E0 b F` and `\b. m b F` end in F,
`\f. c` and `\br. br ...` are not of the form `\x. P x`.

## 6. (G2)

Every production but F is an application.

## 7. (G3)

Let N' be an occurrence in N of a term of G.

- A (C) term reduces to F (F; `I F -> F`; an a-form or c with a (C) slot reaches
  `F Z0 F -> I F -> F` after its label check reduces to T), so replacing it by F is a
  beta reduction in place, and the result is in G by (G1).
- A (Z) term has no weak head normal form: its weak head reduction reaches, after at
  least one step, `m Z0 F` whose weak head reduction begins with that of the slot m,
  again a (Z) term (the label check E0 reaches T and selects c); a (Z) term with a
  shortest weak head reduction to a normal form would have a (Z) slot with a shorter one.
  Every closed subterm of a component element has a weak head normal form (same
  instances, spines and leaves unfold to abstractions, Eq 1 1 reaches T, the right
  comparison Q L5 L6 reaches F, the rest are abstractions and constants; the recursive
  calls `self lA lB` inside components have lA, lB variables), so a (Z) occurrence is not
  inside a component. The operator parts of the productions (`Q A0`, `A0 CL0`,
  `CB0 1 A1`, `E0 c`, `m Z0`, ...) match no production, and the slot is the only place a
  member is admitted; so the (Z) occurrence is N itself or a slot, and a slot admits F.

## 8. Conclusion

G satisfies (G1) to (G3) and contains M, so lambda beta eta + (M = false) is consistent,
and so is the scored test of `tree_same_left_first_differ_right`. The cycle context is
`m Z0 F` (the left-first conjunction): F Z0 F reduces to F, while T Z0 F reduces to Z0,
the right comparison, which is F; so true is not given back (that trap is already a
proved claim).
