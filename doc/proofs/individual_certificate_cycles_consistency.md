# The certificate cycles are consistent for every certificate

Claim. For typing_natural_cycle_three and typing_natural_cycle_five
(`corpora/contest_certificate_cycles.pl`, class certificate_cycle), and for a cycle of
any Count >= 1 members built the same way, all three scored conjectures

    member(1, natural)   \a. M (\i. \w. i a)
    member(2, natural)   \a. M (\i. \w. w y1 y2 a)
    member(2, stream)    \a. M (\i. \w. w y1 a y2)

are consistent with lambda beta. The proof is the one of
`doc/proofs/certificate_ghost_solutions.md` (cited
below as THE CERTIFICATE PROOF): its sets S_W already contain the reduct M' of a cycle
point's term, because its grammar lets a symbol value nest other symbol values to any
depth. Only its section 2, which computes M', is point specific; this document gives
that section for a cycle and checks that the result is generated by the grammar of
section 3 there. Sections 1 and 3 to 7 then apply word for word.

THEOREM. For each cycle point and every closed beta eta normal term W,
lambda beta eta + (M = V_W) is consistent, where V_W = \sel. sel I W.

COROLLARY. As in section 7 of THE CERTIFICATE PROOF, W = \a. \b. \c. c makes the tests
of member(1, natural) and member(2, natural) equal to \a. a, and W = \a. \b. \c. b makes
member(1, natural) and member(2, stream) equal to \a. a; lambda beta is contained in
lambda beta eta, so each of the three claims is consistent.

## 1. The terms

The notation is that of THE CERTIFICATE PROOF, section 1 (T, F, I, Sel1, Sel2, Sel3, N,
object triples `\sel. sel t m b`, `identity_of p = p T`). From the corpus file, with the
object triples, certificate and judgment of H22 and H24 unchanged:

    J   = (\l. JG (l l)) (\r. JG (r r))
    JG  = \judge. \s. (tag s) ((base s) N (judge ((method s) Member[judge]))) ((judge s) F)
    M   = (\judge. judge Member[judge]) J

where Member[judge] is the cycle's member over the variable judge,

    Member[judge] = (\l. MG (l l)) (\r. MG (r r)),   MG = \self. m_0[self]
    m_i[self]     = (true, \that. identity_of (judge that) m_(i+1)[self], false)   i < Count - 1
    m_(Count-1)[self] = (true, \that. identity_of (judge that) self, false)

(`cycle_member_generator/3`, `chained_member/4`, `member_passing/3`: the chain is built
inside MG, the last member passing self). For Count = 1 this is H22's symbol; Count = 2
is H24's pair up to the naming of its members.

## 2. The reduct M' (THE CERTIFICATE PROOF, section 2, for a cycle)

Contract, inside every abstraction, every redex except the two self-applications of each
knot: the selectors, `identity_of`, the triples, JG and MG applied to `l l`. The result is
M' = u0 s0, with u0 = Jh Jh and Jh exactly as in THE CERTIFICATE PROOF,

    Jh = \l. \s. s Sel1 (s Sel3 N (l l (s Sel2 s1))) (l l s F),     s1 = K1 K1,
    u0 = Jh Jh,   s0 = K0 K0,

and, writing u for u0 (in K0) or for `l l` (in K1, bound by Jh),

    K  = \y. tau_0
    tau_i = \sel. sel T (\that. u that T tau_(i+1)) F        i < Count - 1
    tau_(Count-1) = \sel. sel T (\that. u that T (y y)) F

Each step is the one of THE CERTIFICATE PROOF: a triple `(true, Mth, false)` becomes
`\sel. sel T Mth F`, `identity_of (judge that)` becomes `judge that T`, JG applied to
`l l` gives Jh's body, MG applied to `y y` substitutes y y for self. So M ->>b M'.

## 3. M' is generated by the grammar

THE CERTIFICATE PROOF, section 3, has the families

    sigma  ::= y y | K K' | tau
    tau    ::= \sel. sel T Mth F
    Mth    ::= \that. MB[that]
    MB[t]  ::= u t T sigma | Obs[t] T sigma
    K      ::= \y. \sel. sel T (\that. MB_y[that]) F

"where in K the sigma of MB_y (and of the tau inside it, at any depth) may be y y, and
elsewhere sigma is not y y", and u ::= Jh Jh' | Lam | l l (inside a Jh).

- tau_(Count-1) is a tau: its method `\that. u that T (y y)` is a Mth whose MB is
  `u that T sigma` with sigma = y y, allowed because it lies inside a K (at depth Count - 1).
- By descending induction, tau_i for i < Count - 1 is a tau: its method's MB is
  `u that T sigma` with sigma = tau_(i+1), a tau (the third alternative of sigma).
- K0 = \y. tau_0 = \y. \sel. sel T (\that. u0 that T tau_1) F is a K with
  MB_y[that] = `u0 that T tau_1` (for Count = 1, `u0 that T (y y)`); K1 likewise with
  u = l l inside Jh.
- s0 = K0 K0 and s1 = K1 K1 are sigmas (K K'); u0 = Jh Jh is a u.
- So M' = u0 s0 is a member by rule (C) (`u z` with z = s0, a sigma and so a subject).

Nothing else in sections 3 to 7 of THE CERTIFICATE PROOF depends on the point: the
closure under beta and eta (section 4) is proved for the families at any nesting of
sigma; the parse (section 5) reads a tau by its head (`tau` in the table of heads,
first argument T) whatever its method; the collapse lemma (section 6) walks the same
spines. The one place where a cycle differs from H22 and H24, a Mth whose sigma is a
tau holding further taus, is the case "the tau inside it, at any depth" the grammar
states. Hence S_W, for every closed beta eta normal W, contains M' and satisfies the
hypotheses of the lemma, and lambda beta eta + {P = V_W : P in S_W} is consistent and
proves M = M' = V_W.

## 4. The claims

As in section 7 of THE CERTIFICATE PROOF: in that theory M (\i. \w. i a) = I a = a,
M (\i. \w. w y1 y2 a) = W y1 y2 a and M (\i. \w. w y1 a y2) = W y1 a y2, so each test is
\a. a for the W named in the corollary, and each of the three claims of each cycle point
is consistent with lambda beta eta, hence with lambda beta.
