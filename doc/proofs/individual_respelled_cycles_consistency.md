# Consistency of boolean(false) on the three respelled cycles

Claim. For each of `respelled_and_false_self`, `respelled_and_false_identity` and
`respelled_if_false_self` (`corpora/contest_respelled_cycles.pl`), with M the point's
program, the scored conjecture boolean(false), T = \a. M y a (y free), is consistent with
lambda beta: lambda beta + (T = \x. x) is consistent. It suffices that lambda beta eta +
(M = F) is consistent (F = \t. \f. f): that theory proves T = \a. F y a = \a. a, and
lambda beta is contained in lambda beta eta.

## 1. The lemma

The lemma of `individual_path_rebuilt_consistency.md`, Section 1,
with v = F: a set G of closed terms that is closed under one-step beta eta (G1), whose
members other than F are applications (G2), and that is closed under replacing an
occurrence of a member by F (G3), makes lambda beta eta + {N = F : N in G} consistent. We
give such a G containing M.

## 2. The programs

From the corpus file: M = W W I with I = \x. x and

    W = \p. \i. C[p p (A i)]

where the context C and the argument A are, for the three points,

    respelled_and_false_self       C[x] = x F F      A i = i i
    respelled_and_false_identity   C[x] = x F F      A i = (\z. z) i
    respelled_if_false_self        C[x] = x F x      A i = i i

(for if_false both occurrences of the hole hold the same term `p p (A i)`). The two
copies of W in M are the same term up to alpha.

## 3. Components

- WS: the reducts of W. W is beta eta normal except for `(\z. z) i` in the identity case,
  whose reduct replaces `A i` by `i`; so an element of WS is `\p. \i. C[p p A'(i)]` with
  A'(i) = A i, or A'(i) = i in the identity case. No element is an eta redex (the body of
  `\i` ends in F, or, for if_false, in `p p (i i)`; the body of `\p` is an abstraction).
- ARGS(e): for a term e, the least set containing e, closed under beta eta reduction and
  under a |-> A a. ARGS = ARGS(I). Every element of ARGS reduces to I: I does; a reduct of
  a term that reduces to I reduces to I (confluence, I normal); and if a ->> I then
  a a ->> I I -> I and (\z. z) a -> a ->> I. So every element of ARGS has a weak head
  normal form.
- For W1 in WS, the ABSTRACTIONS L(W1): the terms `\i. S`, S an OPEN PRODUCTION of
  Section 4 in the variable i (the grammar read with ARGS(i) for ARGS). Substituting
  a in ARGS for i maps ARGS(i) into ARGS (each element of ARGS(i) is a reduct of some
  A^k(i), which becomes a reduct of A^k(a), in ARGS), so it maps an open production to a
  production.

## 4. The set G

G is the set of closed terms generated by (m ranges over G, the SLOT; for if_false the
two slots of `m F m` are filled independently)

    m ::= W0 W1 a              W0, W1 in WS, a in ARGS               (Z)
        | L0 a                 L0 in L(W1), W1 in WS, a in ARGS      (Z)
        | m F F                (and_false)                            (Z or C, as m)
        | m F m                (if_false)                              (C when both slots are
                                                                       (C), Z otherwise)
        | I F                                                         (C)
        | I m                                                         (Z or C, as m)
        | F                                                           (C)

M = W W I is the first production. A (Z) term is one whose weak head reduction reaches a
(Z) slot (below); a (C) term reduces to F.

## 5. (G1)

A redex lies inside a component (the result stays in it: WS and ARGS are reduct sets; in
L0 = `\i. S` a step inside S is a step of the open production S, by the same table read
with a variable), inside a slot (induction on the term), or at the root of a production:

    W0 W1 a      -> (\i. C[W1 W1 A'(i)]) a      an L0 a, the body S = C[W1 W1 A'(i)]
                                                 being `(W1 W1 A'(i)) F F` resp.
                                                 `(W1 W1 A'(i)) F (W1 W1 A'(i))`, an open
                                                 production with slot `W1 W1 A'(i)`
    L0 a         -> S[i := a]                    a production (Section 3)
    m F F        -> (F F) F = I F                only when m = F (the only abstraction in
                                                 G): F F -> \f. f = I
    m F m'       -> I m'                         only when m = F
    I F -> F,    I m -> m

In the open body S, `W1 W1` is a redex whose reduct unfolds the abstraction again:
`W1 W1 A'(i) -> (\i'. C[W1 W1 A'(i')]) A'(i) -> C[W1 W1 A'(A'(i))]`, the productions
`L0 a` and C[slot] with an argument of ARGS(i); so the open productions are closed under
reduction by this table. There is no eta redex at a production: `\i. S` does not end in i
(S ends in F, or in its second slot, a term with head W1).

## 6. (G2)

Every production but F is an application.

## 7. (G3)

Let N' be an occurrence in N (both in G) of a member.

- (C) terms reduce to F: F; `I F -> F`; `I m ->> F` when m is (C); `m F F ->> F F F ->
  I F -> F` when m is (C); `m F m' ->> F F m' -> I m' -> m' ->> F` when both slots are
  (C). Replacing a (C) occurrence by F is then a reduction in place, and the result is
  in G by (G1).
- (Z) terms have no weak head normal form: `W0 W1 a` reaches `L0 a` and then
  `C[W1 W1 a']` with its first slot `W1 W1 a'` in head position, a (Z) production;
  `L0 a` reaches the same; `m F F` and `I m` put their (Z) slot in head position or
  reduce to it; `m F m'` puts m in head position when m is (Z), and when m is (C) it
  reduces to its second slot m', then (Z). So the weak head reduction of a (Z) term reaches, after at
  least one step or as a proper head, a (Z) term, and a (Z) term with the shortest weak
  head reduction to a normal form would have one with a shorter reduction, a
  contradiction.

  Every closed subterm of a component element has a weak head normal form: WS and
  L(W1) hold abstractions; ARGS elements reduce to I; the closed subterms of an open
  body S are W1, `W1 W1` (which reduces to an abstraction), F and the elements of ARGS,
  since every occurrence of `W1 W1 a'` there has an argument in ARGS(i), open. The parts
  of a production other than its slots are components, F, I, and the operator parts
  `W0 W1`, `m F` and `m F` of `m F m'`: `W0 W1` reduces to an abstraction; `m F` is a
  member only if it is a production, and the only production of the form `X F` is `I F`,
  with X = I, which is not a member (an abstraction other than F). So a (Z) occurrence
  is N itself or a slot, and a slot admits F.

## 8. Conclusion

G satisfies (G1) to (G3) and contains M, so lambda beta eta + (M = F) is consistent, and
so is lambda beta + (T = \x. x) for the scored test of each of the three points. The
argument uses of A only that A a reduces to I when a does, and of C only its shape; it is
the false_only argument of `individual_false_only_consistency.md` with the fixpoint's
self-application `p p` read at a growing argument.
